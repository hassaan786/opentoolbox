All tools

Privacy

Short version: your files stay on your device.

What happens to your files

Every tool runs inside your browser tab. Images, audio, video, PDFs and text you open are read by JavaScript on your own device and are never sent to a server. When you close the tab, they are gone.

What the site downloads

AI tools need model files. The first time you use one, your browser downloads the open model weights from Hugging Face (and, for the chat tools, a small runtime file from GitHub). These downloads contain no information about your files. Your browser caches them so later visits work offline.

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Some runtime files load from the jsDelivr CDN.

Accounts, cookies and tracking

There are no accounts. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad scripts.

Source code

Everything is open source, so you can check these claims yourself: github.com/hassaan786/opentoolbox.
